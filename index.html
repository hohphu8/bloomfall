A LITTLE FIELD GUIDE

{dialogTitle()}

Swap neighboring pieces to make a horizontal or vertical line of at least three. Diagonals don't count.

  1. A match earns 20 points per piece.
  2. Falling pieces can match again. Each cascade adds a multiplier: ×2, ×3, and so on.
  3. You have 24 successful moves. Swaps without a match are free.

Reach 1,800 points to make your garden bloom. Keep playing to finish your 24 moves. If no matches are possible, the garden reshuffles itself.

Keyboard-friendly, too.
Tab into the board. Arrow keys move focus; Enter or Space selects a piece. Select a neighbor to swap. Escape clears selection.

Everything stays on this device. No accounts, trackers or network requests during play.

Start this same garden from the beginning? Your current moves will be cleared. Your personal best stays.

A new garden replaces the one saved on this device. Daily gardens use the same seed for everyone, changing at midnight UTC.
